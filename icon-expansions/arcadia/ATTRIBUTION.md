# Arcadia Science organism icons: attribution

**Source:** [Free organism illustration library](https://arcadia-science.github.io/organism-illustration-library/) of
Arcadia Science, version 1.2 of 2026-01-28, archived on Zenodo
([doi:10.5281/zenodo.18395998](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18395998)). The record names Arcadia Science as the
creator and Audrey Bell, Megan Hochstrasser, Robert Roth and Greg Wright as contributors; the gallery credits every
image to Arcadia Science.

**License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), for every file of the library. See `LICENSE.txt`.

**How Arcadia Science asks to be credited** (read on https://zenodo.org/records/18395998 on 2026-11-14):

> All images are released under CC0, which has no usage restrictions. That said, feel free to cite the associated pub,
> "A free, open-access library of high-quality organism illustrations for science communication," to help others find
> this resource.

So no credit is required, and SiliCoDraw adds no credit line for these icons by itself. A citation is appreciated:
Bell A, Hochstrasser ML, Roth R, Wright G (2025). A free, open-access library of high-quality organism illustrations
for science communication. The Stacks. doi:10.57844/arcadia-4gbv-pzkv. Page → Copy full icon credits writes one line
per icon with the library's DOI.

**Drawings adapted from PhyloPic:** Arcadia Science adapted ten of its drawings from PhyloPic silhouettes and names the
PhyloPic uploader of each in the library's Notes field. All ten are in this pack, each with that note in its credits
(yellow fever mosquito, white button mushroom, filamentous fungus *Aspergillus nidulans*, common marmoset, Australian
ghostshark, *Diplonema papillatum*, rhesus macaque, chimpanzee, *Perkinsus marinus*, zebra finch). On PhyloPic every
image of that species (or genus) by the uploader the library names is CC0 1.0 or Public Domain Mark 1.0 (the images are
listed in `LICENSE.txt`; for the chimpanzee, the ghostshark, the macaque, the mosquito and the mushroom the outline was
compared with the drawing and matches). PhyloPic's CC BY 3.0 chimpanzees and CC BY-SA 3.0 ghostshark come from
other uploaders than the ones the library names.

**What SiliCoDraw changed:** the SVG files of the record's ZIP were converted to SiliCoDraw's icon format with
`dev/icon-import`, each organism as its three-color drawing ("tricolor + stroke") and as its silhouette: fitted into a
100 × 100 box, style sheets written out as attributes, metadata and editor data removed, coordinates rounded, and the
two main colors made recolorable in the editor. One embedded picture (a soft shading inside the mouse drawing) was
removed after the drawing without it was found to look the same. Every icon was drawn and compared with the original
on a white and on a dark page; all of them passed. The library's PNG pictures and GIF animations are not part of the
pack. The groups in the Add panel (animals, fungi, plants and algae, protists, bacteria and viruses) are SiliCoDraw's;
the library has none.

SiliCoDraw is not affiliated with or endorsed by Arcadia Science, and uses no Arcadia Science logo.
