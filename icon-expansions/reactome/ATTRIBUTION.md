# Reactome Icon Library icons: attribution

**Source:** the [Reactome Icon Library](https://reactome.org/icon-lib) of [Reactome](https://reactome.org), the
open-source, curated pathway knowledgebase. The files were taken from Reactome's official GitHub repository
[reactome/reactome_illustrations](https://github.com/reactome/reactome_illustrations) (folder `icons/`, commit
`f3d331c`, Reactome release 99), where every icon has a metadata file naming its designer and the Reactome curator who
checked it. Most icons were drawn by Cristoffer Sevilla (graphsolutions.co.uk) for Reactome; others by Sam Norwitz,
Junaid Haque and David Eccles. The table below names the designer of each icon; its Page column links the icon's file
in that repository.

**License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), as Reactome's License Agreement states for its
"Pathway Illustrations, Icon Library, Art, and Branding Materials" (Section 1(a)). See `LICENSE.txt`.

**How Reactome asks to be credited** (License Agreement, https://reactome.org/license, Section 1(a), as Reactome's
website source had it on 2026-11-14):

> User may copy, modify, and distribute these materials, provided that appropriate credit is given, a link to the
> license is included, and any modifications made by User are clearly indicated.

and, about the library (Reactome news, 2018-10-25): "To acknowledge the community engagement, each icon is attributed to
the author through a metadata file linking to a portfolio and/or ORCID id."

So an icon is credited with its name and designer, the Reactome Icon Library and the license (with its link), and the
credit says that the icon was changed, for example:
*Icons: Neuron by Sam Norwitz, reactome.org/icon-lib, CC BY 4.0, adapted.* SiliCoDraw writes such a line on the page when
one of these icons is used. With more than three of them on a page the line names only the Reactome Icon Library; Page →
Copy full icon credits then gives the complete list (name, designer, page, license with its link) for the figure legend
or the acknowledgments. The library states no year for an icon, so the credits carry none.

**What SiliCoDraw changed:** each SVG file was converted to SiliCoDraw's icon format with `dev/icon-import`: fitted into
a 100 × 100 box, gradient, mask and clip-path ids renamed, coordinates rounded, and the two main colors made recolorable
in the editor. Names are kept as Reactome writes them (mRNA, tRNA), in American spelling where it differs (the
library's own spelling stays the title the credits print).

**What was left out** (2,571 icons in the library, 645 in this pack):

- the categories Arrow and Background (54: arrows and whole pathway pictures, not icons);
- the label badges (1,561): proteins, compounds, drugs and complexes drawn as a pill, hexagon, circle or tag with
  their name written in as outlines; they are the same few shapes told apart only by the letters, and SiliCoDraw's
  chip and text elements make such labels as real text that can be edited and checked for size;
- six drawings with a label written into the drawing (CLR, LAT, SHR, 5mC-DNA, PANX1 heptamer, Scavenger receptor
  class G), for the same reason;
- drawings that are only other colors of a kept one (263: sugars of the SNFG symbol set drawn alike, genes drawn as one DNA
  strand with a colored band, receptors and transporters of one family drawn alike, organelles and particles drawn as
  the same shaded sphere); SiliCoDraw recolors icons, and the
  names of the drawings left out still find the one kept. A drawing whose color or detail tells what it is stays an
  icon of its own: the sugars arabinose, lyxose and fructose, the generic sugars, nucleotides and damaged bases, the
  T cell stages and subtypes (immature, progenitor, naive CD4+ and CD8+, regulatory), the regulatory B cell, CTLA4
  beside CD28, the COPI and COPII vesicles beside the exosome, preNOTCH beside NOTCH1, and uridylated beside
  adenylated mRNA;
- drawings that did not come out the same after conversion (25, among them the square and octagon shapes that fill
  their whole frame), very long thin strips (7) and drawings over 300 KB after conversion (10 detailed virus and tissue
  drawings).

Reactome's logo and branding are not icons of the library and are not in the pack. The icons show no identifiable real
people.

SiliCoDraw is not affiliated with or endorsed by Reactome.
