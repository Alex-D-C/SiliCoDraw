# SciDraw icons: attribution

**Source:** [SciDraw](https://scidraw.io), a community repository of scientific drawings. Every drawing was made by the
authors named in the table below (all the authors SciDraw lists for it); SciDraw gives each one a DOI.

**License:** per drawing, the one its author chose: CC BY 4.0 (credit required) or CC0 1.0 (no credit required). See
`LICENSE.txt` and the License column below.

**How SciDraw asks to be credited** (read on https://scidraw.io/terms, section "Usage", on 2026-11-11):

> Please acknowledge the contribution of the image's authors and of SciDraw in the acknowledgements section of your work.
> You can use the DOI displayed below each drawing to cite the illustration properly.

with the example `Author Name(s). (Year). "Illustration Title". SciDraw. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx`.

So a CC BY drawing is credited with its authors and DOI, SciDraw and the license are named, and the credit says that
the drawing was changed (CC BY 4.0 asks for that), for example:
*Icons: Mouse by A. Name (doi:10.5281/zenodo.0000000), scidraw.io, CC BY 4.0, adapted.* SiliCoDraw writes such a line on
the page when one of these icons is used. With more than three such drawings on a page the line names only SciDraw;
Page → Copy full icon credits then gives the complete list (authors, title, DOI, license with its link) for the figure
legend or the acknowledgments. SciDraw states no year for a drawing (the "Cite" button of its pages prints the year in
which it is pressed), so the credits here carry none; add the year your journal's style asks for.

**What SiliCoDraw changed:** each SVG file was converted to SiliCoDraw's icon format with `dev/icon-import`: fitted into
a 100 × 100 box, style sheets written out as attributes, text, metadata and editor data removed, coordinates rounded, a
white page rectangle behind a drawing removed, and the two main colors made recolorable in the editor. Drawings with
embedded pictures, drawings that did not come out the same after conversion, drawings over 60 KB and duplicates were
left out. Also left out: SciDraw's own logo and icon, and drawings of identifiable real people (a Creative Commons
license covers the drawing, not the rights of the person shown). Some author names are the placeholders the repository
itself shows ("Scidraw", "Anonymous Artist"); they are printed as given. Where two drawings have the same title, the
editor's library numbers the later one ("Mouse 2"); the table and the credits use the title as SciDraw has it.

The uploads are certified by their uploaders ("it is the users' responsibility to ensure that they have the right to
share the content"); SiliCoDraw has not checked them one by one.
