# NIH BioArt Source icons: attribution

**Source:** [NIH BioArt Source](https://bioart.niaid.nih.gov) of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious
Diseases (NIAID), U.S. National Institutes of Health. Every entry names its creator, or the author its "Cite This
Entry" names (table below); the site's credit line for its own drawings is "Courtesy of NIAID".

**Terms:** every icon here comes from an entry the site labels "Licensing: Public Domain". The site's terms say its
content is "free for any use, including educational, research, informational, and commercial purposes". The entries it
labels CC BY 4.0 (tissue drawings, most of the Human Reference Atlas, whose labels, scale bars and badge are drawn
into them) are not in this pack. See `LICENSE.txt` (the site's Terms and Conditions as retrieved).

**How NIH BioArt Source asks to be credited** (read on https://bioart.niaid.nih.gov/faqs and
https://bioart.niaid.nih.gov/terms on 2026-11-13):

> If the entry is licensed as "Public Domain" citation is appreciated but not required.

> Credit directly below image on web, presentation, or print:
> "Illustration from NIAID NIH BioArt Source (bioart.niaid.nih.gov/bioart/###)"

So no credit is required, and SiliCoDraw adds no credit line for these icons by itself. A credit is appreciated:
Page → Copy full icon credits writes one line per icon with the entry's number.

**What SiliCoDraw changed:** one file per drawing was converted to SiliCoDraw's icon format with `dev/icon-import`
(an entry's other files are the same drawing in other colors, -blue, -grey, -bw, -red-hue and so on; SiliCoDraw
recolors icons itself, so they were left out after their names, captions and shapes were compared): fitted into a
100 × 100 box, style sheets written out as attributes, color inversions inside masks written into the colors, drop
shadows, glows and pattern fills kept, text, metadata and editor data removed, embedded pictures that barely show
removed, coordinates rounded, and the two main colors made recolorable in the editor. Every icon was drawn and compared
with the original on a white and on a dark page; left out were drawings whose look depends on an embedded picture,
drawings that did not come out the same after conversion (lettering, for example), drawings over 300 KB after
conversion or over 3 MB as downloaded, drawings of the same shapes as another entry (color versions uploaded as entries
of their own, such as a VSV particle and a plasmid drawn again in other colors, and a tick drawn again: their names still find
the icon that was kept), two shipping canes with their labels drawn in, and brushes, swatches and templates without an SVG file.

**Colorways:** 11 drawings that are the same shapes as another drawing of this pack in other colors are no icons of their own: they are colorways of 4 icons (the icon in their colors, one click away in SiliCoDraw's side panel; their names still find it, and figures that used them keep drawing them). The drawings themselves are unchanged. A drawing that shows another state of a thing is an icon of its own, not a colorway (a flask and a vial empty and filled, a monitor with its screen on and blank, heterozygous and homozygous alleles, a person with and without a lab coat, a blood smear unstained and Wright-Giemsa stained, a plain plasmid and one with a segment highlighted).
