# NIH BioArt Source icons: attribution

**Source:** [NIH BioArt Source](https://bioart.niaid.nih.gov) of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious
Diseases (NIAID), U.S. National Institutes of Health. Every entry names its creator (table below); the site's credit
line for them is "Courtesy of NIAID".

**Terms:** only entries the site labels "Licensing: Public Domain" are in this pack. The site's terms say its content is
"free for any use, including educational, research, informational, and commercial purposes". See `LICENSE.txt` (the
site's Terms and Conditions as retrieved; the site gives no other legal text for "Public Domain").

**How NIH BioArt Source asks to be credited** (read on https://bioart.niaid.nih.gov/faqs and
https://bioart.niaid.nih.gov/terms on 2026-11-11):

> If the entry is licensed as "Public Domain" citation is appreciated but not required.

> Credit directly below image on web, presentation, or print:
> "Illustration from NIAID NIH BioArt Source (bioart.niaid.nih.gov/bioart/###)"

So no credit is required, and SiliCoDraw adds no credit line by itself for these icons. A credit is appreciated; Page →
Copy full icon credits writes one per icon with the entry's number.

**What SiliCoDraw changed:** one color variant of each entry was converted to SiliCoDraw's icon format with
`dev/icon-import`: fitted into a 100 × 100 box, style sheets written out as attributes, text, metadata and editor data
removed, coordinates rounded, and the two main colors made recolorable in the editor. Entries with embedded pictures,
entries that did not come out the same after conversion (filters, lettering), entries over 60 KB and near-duplicate
color variants were left out; so were brushes, swatches and templates (no SVG) and the 28 entries labeled "CC-BY".

SiliCoDraw is not affiliated with or endorsed by NIH or NIAID, and uses no NIH or NIAID logo.
