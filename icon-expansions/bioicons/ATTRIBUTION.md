# Bioicons icons: attribution

**Source:** [Bioicons](https://bioicons.com), a free community library of scientific icons curated by Simon Dürr. The
files were taken from its official GitHub repository [duerrsimon/bioicons](https://github.com/duerrsimon/bioicons)
(folder `static/icons/`, commit `d29e766`), where every icon is filed as `<license>/<category>/<creator>/<name>.svg`:
the folder names its creator and its license ("The folders are organized in `license/category/author`",
CONTRIBUTING.md). The creator of each icon is in the table below as Bioicons shows it (the folder name, with `_` as a
space; the separators of a folder name read back where the folder could not hold the name: `B--Gideon-Bergheim` = B.
Gideon Bergheim, `David-Eccles--gringer-` = David Eccles (gringer); one person's several folders, which the repository's
`authors.json` links to the same page, as one name). Its Page column links the icon's file in that repository.

**License:** per icon, the one its creator chose: CC0 1.0 (no credit required), CC BY 4.0 (credit required),
CC BY-SA 4.0 (credit required, share-alike) or MIT (the copyright and license notice must be kept; SiliCoDraw credits
these icons like CC BY ones). See `LICENSE.txt` and the License column below. In this pack: 262 icons under CC0, 126
under CC BY 4.0, 26 under CC BY-SA 4.0 and 36 under MIT.

**How Bioicons asks to be credited** (README.md of the repository, as it was on 2026-11-14):

> Please always cite the individual icons and their respective license. Credit to the platform itself is appreciated but
> not necessary.

and: "Note that if the icon is licensed as CC BY you still need attribute and state the modifications made." Its example
credit has the form "<name> icon by <creator> <link> is licensed under <license> <license link>".

So an icon under CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 or MIT is credited with its name, its creator and its license, and the credit
says that the icon was changed, for example: *Icons: Neutrophil by Helicase 11, bioicons.com, CC BY 4.0, adapted.*
SiliCoDraw writes such a line on the page when one of these icons is used. With more than three of them on a page the
line names only Bioicons; Page → Copy full icon credits then gives the complete list (name, creator, page, license with
its link) for the figure legend or the acknowledgments. Bioicons states no year for an icon, so the credits carry none.

**Share-alike:** the 26 icons under CC BY-SA 4.0 (Stefano Vianello's fish development stages, some receptors and cells
by Helicase 11, and a few others; the License column says which) stay under CC BY-SA 4.0 after SiliCoDraw's changes, and
what is made from them must be shared under the same license.

**What SiliCoDraw changed:** each SVG file was converted to SiliCoDraw's icon format with `dev/icon-import`: fitted into
a 100 × 100 box, style sheets written out as attributes, small text labels (`<text>`), metadata and editor data
removed, embedded pictures that barely show removed, the white page that a drawing traced from a picture kept behind it
removed, coordinates rounded, and the two main colors made recolorable in the editor. Each icon was drawn and compared with the original on a white and on a dark page. Names are made from the
file names in American spelling; where the library gives an icon another name (an abbreviation spelled out, a typo, a
number), the file's own name stays the title that the credits print.

**Colorways:** 1 drawing that is the same shapes as another drawing of this pack in other colors is no icon of its own: it is a colorway of 1 icon (the icon in its colors, one click away in SiliCoDraw's side panel; its names still find it, and figures that used it keep drawing it). The drawings themselves are unchanged. A drawing that shows another state of a thing is an icon of its own, not a colorway (a GFP-labeled and an unlabeled colony, a Falcon tube with and without medium, the nucleotides T and U), and so is a drawing whose color is its meaning (the fluorescent bead in blue, green, orange, red and yellow: the color is the fluorophore).

**What was left out** (2,830 icons in the repository, 455 converted; 454 icons in this pack, since 1 drawing that is another icon in other colors became its colorway, see Colorways above):

- drawings Bioicons re-hosts from other illustration libraries (2,041: the drawings of a medical art library filed under
  CC BY 3.0, DBCLS TogoTV and SwissBioPics); SiliCoDraw takes such libraries from their own source, under their own
  terms;
- logos and brand marks (49): the `bsd` folder (software logos), the logos of software, databases and companies filed
  as icons, a vendor's own renderings of its branded instruments, and drawings of branded instruments or products (a
  GPU with its maker's name, a camera with its model name, a flow cytometer, an HPLC, a chromatography system, a plate
  reader, a sequencer and an NMR spectrometer named after their makers);
- one icon filed without a creator, and one whose license the original source states otherwise (a Morse potential
  drawing that Wikimedia Commons has under CC BY-SA 4.0 by another author);
- drawings whose labels or symbols were drawn as text that the conversion removes, so that they would lose their
  meaning (chemical structures without their atom labels, charges without their signs), and drawings that are mostly
  labels (a substitution matrix, a scoring matrix, a one-hot encoding table, a heart diagram with its labels, a mosquito
  pair labeled Male and Female, a labeled plant cell diagram), and drawings left with a few of their labels (a U-tube
  without its electrode signs, a well plate with one row and one column still numbered), 19 in all;
- one drawing with parts that are embedded pictures (three of its five bacteria), which the conversion leaves out, and one
  drawing traced closely from a photograph that its file carries without saying where the photograph is from (a shark);
- two drawings that a pack already shipped with SiliCoDraw has (an alpaca and a set of beakers, the same drawing in
  SciDraw);
- drawings that are only other colors of a kept one (54: the colors of an antibody, a pill, a bottle, a PCR
  plate, flasks of other sizes, fungal stages in other colors, the hazard pictograms drawn a second time, with and
  without their caption, beside the official GHS set of UNECE); SiliCoDraw recolors icons, and the names of those left
  out still find the one kept;
- drawings that did not come out the same after conversion (166, most of them because text was part of the drawing:
  the amino acids, nucleotides and labeled diagrams), drawings that are a picture rather than vectors (15), very long
  thin strips (12), drawings over 300 KB after conversion or over 3 MB as files (11), one drawing that holds another SVG
  document, and one exact copy of a kept drawing.

The icons show no identifiable real people.

SiliCoDraw is not affiliated with or endorsed by Bioicons or its contributors.
