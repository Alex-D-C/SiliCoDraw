# Open Crop Icons: attribution

**Source:** Open Crop Icons, "an openly licenced icon set of commonly cultivated plants", an icon set of the OpenFarm
project, from its GitHub repository [openfarmcc/open-crop-icons](https://github.com/openfarmcc/open-crop-icons) at commit
`d41f1197a14bb82f12037dc47585152ae1e3074f` (2022-12-17, the last commit of its branch `mainline`), read from GitHub's ZIP
of that commit. The icons came into the repository as pull requests of community contributors; the repository names no
creator per icon, so this pack names none. The OpenFarm project itself shut down in 2025; the icon repository stays
published on GitHub.

**License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication): "These are released under a CC0 license. They're public domain
works so use them without restriction." (the repository's README); its package.json declares `"license": "CC0-1.0"`.
See `LICENSE.txt`.

**How Open Crop Icons asks to be credited** (read in the README on 2026-11-14):

> A credit somewhere to help other people find them would be in good spirit, but is not required.

So no credit is required, and SiliCoDraw adds no credit line for these icons. Page → Copy full icon credits gives a line
naming the set for anyone who wants to give one: *Open Crop Icons, OpenFarm contributors
(github.com/openfarmcc/open-crop-icons), CC0 1.0*.

**What SiliCoDraw changed:** the SVG files were converted to SiliCoDraw's icon format with `dev/icon-import`, one drawing
per crop (`icons/<crop>/<crop>.svg`; the PNG pictures and the 24, 32 and 64 px copies of the corn and pumpkin icons are
not included). Each icon was fitted into a 100 × 100 box, its style sheet written out as attributes, its coordinates
rounded and its two main colors made recolorable. Every icon was drawn and compared with the original on a white and on a
dark page. Names are the folder names in words, with misspellings corrected (Artichoke for "atrichoke",
Marjoram, Chanterelle, Portobello, Brussels Sprout, Lacinato Kale, Yukon Gold Potato, Fatalii Pepper, Chile de Árbol
Pepper, Cherry Belle Radish), apostrophes and accents added (Bird's Eye Chili, Bishop's Crown Pepper, Lion's Mane, St.
John's Wort, Jalapeño, Ají Dulce), and a word added where the folder name says too little alone (Apple for
"generic-apple", Iceberg Lettuce, Trumpet Mushroom, Chives for "onion-chive"); the folder names stay search words. The
groups in the Add panel are SiliCoDraw's (fruits and nuts, grains and field crops, peppers and chilies, fruiting
vegetables, leaf and stem vegetables, roots, tubers and onions, beans and peas, herbs and medicinal plants, mushrooms,
flowers and other plants), since the repository has none.

**Colorways:** 1 drawing that is the same shapes as another drawing of this pack in other colors is no icon of its own: it is a colorway of 1 icon (the icon in its colors, one click away in SiliCoDraw's side panel; its names still find it, and figures that used it keep drawing it). The drawings themselves are unchanged.

**One drawing for its colors:** SiliCoDraw recolors icons, so a drawing the repository has again in other colors is in
the pack once, and the names of the others find it: the carrots (orange kept; purple, red, white and yellow), the onions
(red kept, as Onion; white and yellow), the cabbages (green kept, as Cabbage; red), the pointed cabbage (red), the beans
(Blue Lake kept, as Green Bean; purple pod and wax), the chards (Swiss kept; red and rainbow), the Scotch bonnets (orange
kept; red and yellow), the curly kale (red), the cauliflower (purple and yellow), the Anjou and Bartlett pears (green
kept; red), the beet (golden), the habanero (black) and the Golden Delicious apple (Granny Smith).
A drawing of another ripeness or of another species is an icon of its own, even where only its colors differ: the bell
peppers (green, red, orange and yellow), the Tabasco and serrano peppers (green and red), the oyster mushrooms (blue,
pink, white and yellow), the salsify beside the black salsify, the Japanese yam beside the sweet potato, and the
nectarine beside the peach.

**Left out:** the cannabis plant and the psilocybin mushroom (by choice of SiliCoDraw), and the cantaloupe, whose
conversion did not pass the comparison with the original (about 12 % of its pixels differed: part of its netting is
drawn through nested clips of its drawing program).
