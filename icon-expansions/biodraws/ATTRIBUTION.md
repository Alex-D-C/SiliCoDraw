# BioDraws icons: attribution

**Source:** [BioDraws](https://biodraws.com) (biodraws.com). The site names no creator per illustration; the credit goes
to BioDraws.

**License:** Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0), for every illustration. See
`LICENSE.txt`.

**How BioDraws asks to be credited** (read on https://biodraws.com/faq/, "How to cite the website?", on 2026-11-11):

> If you have used illustrations from our website for your thesis, presentations, publications, or posters, please cite
> us by mentioning "Images are obtained from BioDraws.com." in the figure legend.

Its terms (https://biodraws.com/wpautoterms/terms-and-conditions/) add what the license asks: "You must give appropriate
credit to the original creator, provide a link to the license, and indicate if changes were made." The line SiliCoDraw
writes on the page is therefore: *Images are obtained from BioDraws.com (CC BY-SA 4.0), adapted*.

**Share-alike:** the same terms say: "If you remix, transform, or build upon the material, you must distribute your
contributions under the same license as the original." Changed icons stay under CC BY-SA 4.0, and this can extend to a
figure built with them. Check your journal's terms before using these icons in a paper.

**What SiliCoDraw changed:** each SVG file was converted to SiliCoDraw's icon format with `dev/icon-import`: fitted into
a 100 × 100 box, styles written out as attributes, text, metadata and editor data removed, coordinates rounded, and the
two main colors made recolorable in the editor. Illustrations with embedded pictures, illustrations that did not come
out the same after conversion (mostly ones with lettering), illustrations over 60 KB and duplicates were left out. The
site's logos are not part of the pack. The converted icons remain under CC BY-SA 4.0.
